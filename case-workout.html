Every movement explained, tracked, and visible to the coach

01 / The Problem

Training plans lived on printed sheets — a list of exercise names with no photo of the machine, no explanation of the movement, and no way to tell which muscle was actually meant to be working. Trainers had no visibility into whether a client trained at all between sessions, let alone whether they did it right.

02 / The Approach

A workout plan only works if the person doing it understands it without the trainer standing next to them. So every exercise needed to carry its own explanation — what it looks like, how to do it, what it's working — and the trainer needed a way to see activity without having to ask.
