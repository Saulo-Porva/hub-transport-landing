01 / The Problem

Accounting teams were distributing work the way small teams always do — by memory and by asking around. It worked until it didn't: someone got overloaded while another sat idle, deadlines slipped without anyone noticing until a client called, and there was no record of who owned what once a task moved between people.

02 / Before

  • ✕Tasks assigned informally, by asking around who had time
  • ✕No visibility into who was overloaded and who was idle
  • ✕Deadlines slipped silently until a client called to ask
  • ✕No record of who owned what once a task changed hands

03 / The System

The fix wasn't a to-do list — teams already had those and they weren't the problem. It was visibility: making workload and ownership visible to everyone at once, so distribution could be a fair, informed decision instead of a habit. Every task needed an owner and a deadline from the moment it was created, not after it slipped.
