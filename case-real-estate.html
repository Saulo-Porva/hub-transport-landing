AI Real Estate — A First Response That Never Sleeps

Leads stop going cold just because it's midnight or a Sunday.

01 / The Problem

Most real estate leads come in outside office hours — browsing listings after work, on weekends, whenever the idea of moving crosses someone's mind. Every one of those leads waited for a human to be available, and by the time someone replied, plenty had already moved on to the next listing, or the next agency.

02 / Before

  • ✕Every lead waited for a human to be free, any hour they wrote in
  • ✕By the time someone replied, plenty had moved to the next listing
  • ✕Booking a visit meant a back-and-forth to find a slot
  • ✕Price and availability answers depended on who picked up

03 / The System

The agent didn't need to replace the human — it needed to handle the part that doesn't require judgment: answering what's available, at what price, and getting a visit on the calendar. So it's wired directly into the listings data and the agent's real calendar, not a script reciting canned answers.
