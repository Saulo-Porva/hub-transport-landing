Featured case — Fleet Operations

Fleet Control — Trip Tracking & Invoicing

Turning paper trip logs into a system that closes invoices in weeks, not months.

Client

Road transport companies

Sector

Logistics

Stack

Mobile app + dispatch dashboard

Status

Live

60 days → same month

Invoicing cycle, before and after

01 / The Problem

Drivers were logging trips on paper — one sheet per month, filled in by hand, often illegible, sometimes lost before it reached the office. By the time someone typed it up, reconciled fuel receipts, and matched it against what was actually loaded, invoices routinely took up to 60 days to close. Nobody had a clear view of which trailer was loaded with what, or where a given vehicle was mid-route.

02 / Before

  • ✕One paper sheet per driver per month — handwritten, often illegible
  • ✕Fuel and workshop receipts reconciled by hand at month's end
  • ✕No live view of what was loaded on a trailer, or where it was mid-route
  • ✕Dispatch assigned trips from memory, not from actual vehicle fit

03 / The System

Instead of digitizing the paper form as-is, I mapped the actual sequence a driver goes through — start the trip, load, drive, unload, log fuel, close the month — and built a mobile flow around that sequence, not around what a spreadsheet needed. Dispatch got a separate view: a board to allocate the fleet to whichever trip fits best, not just whichever comes next.
