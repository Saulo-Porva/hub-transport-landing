Health Anywhere — Finding a Doctor on the Patient's Terms

One app connecting patients to nearby doctors, however they want to be seen.

Sector

Healthcare

Stack

Patient + doctor mobile app

Status

Live

3 ways to be seen

In-office, video, or home visit — in one app

01 / The Problem

Patients looking for a doctor nearby had no single place to check who was available, how far away they were, or whether that doctor even offered the kind of visit they needed — in person, by video, or at home. Booking meant a phone call during office hours, or nothing at all outside them.

02 / Before

  • ✕No single place to see which nearby doctors were actually available
  • ✕Visit type (in-person, video, home) was never clear before calling
  • ✕Booking only possible by phone, only during office hours
  • ✕No direct way to message a doctor before committing to a visit

03 / The System

The design question was never "how do we list doctors" — it was "how does a patient decide, in under a minute, whether this doctor is the right fit right now." That meant distance, availability, and visit type all had to be visible together, with messaging built in so a patient isn't stuck guessing before they commit to a booking.
